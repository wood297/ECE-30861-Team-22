This service collects a product release's EU Cyber Resilience Act (Annex VII) documents from the places a company keeps them, and bundles them. It's a course project (ECE 30861, Team 22) running in Amazon Web Services' us-east-2 (Ohio) region.

What we store, and where

WhatWhereHow long
Your name, email address and password (and an authenticator app, if you add one) Amazon Cognito. We never see your passwordUntil you delete your account
Organizations, who belongs to them and their roles, pending invites (by email address), connection settings, products and releases Amazon DynamoDB, keyed by organizationUntil the organization or your account is deleted; invites expire after 7 days
Connection tokens (GitHub, Confluence, Jenkins) AWS Systems Manager Parameter Store, encrypted with AWS KMS. Never shown again after you enter one Until the connection or organization is deleted
Bundles, and the files fetched for themThe server's memory only An hour for bundles, 10 minutes for fetched files, and never past a restart
Server logsThe serverThey record the organization, connection and outcome of each fetch, never a token or a file's contents

Classification by Amazon Bedrock

When a file's name or first heading doesn't settle which document it is, its text is sent to a model on Amazon Bedrock in us-east-2 to classify it. AWS states that Bedrock doesn't store prompts or use them to train models. Each company can make at most 200 such calls a day.

Who can see your company's data

Only your organization's members, through their own sign-in. Other companies can't see your connections, products, releases or bundles. Requests without a sign-in only ever see the shared course test data.

Deleting your data

  • An organization: an admin deletes it from its members page. Its tokens are deleted first, then every record.
  • Your account: delete it from your account page. This removes your sign-in, your memberships, and organizations where you're the only member.
